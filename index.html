HexFall — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

HexFall is a strategy game for iOS and Android, made by a single independent developer in the United Kingdom. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: HexFall has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you do in the game is profiled, sold, or shared with advertisers. Apart from an anonymous identifier created when you first open the app (explained below), nothing about you leaves your device unless you play online or choose to post scores to the Daily Challenge leaderboard.

Information stored on your device

HexFall saves your progress on your device only, using the operating system's standard app storage. That includes:

  • the player name you choose;
  • campaign progress, unlocked content and achievements;
  • game statistics;
  • game settings, such as sound and haptics preferences;
  • your Daily Challenge personal bests, streak and medal tally;
  • whether the full-game unlock has been purchased.

This information is not transmitted to the developer, and deleting the app deletes all of it. The only exception is your player name, which is sent to other players if you play online, and published if you join the Daily Challenge leaderboard — both described below.

The anonymous identifier

The first time you open HexFall, the app creates an anonymous account with Google Firebase (Anonymous Authentication) and stores it on your device, so that your device keeps the same identifier between sessions. It is used only for online play and the Daily Challenge. It is not linked to your Google or Apple account, your email address, or any real-world identity, and it is not used to identify you outside the game. Creating it, like any internet connection, makes your IP address visible to Google's servers; it is not used to determine your location.

Playing on one device

The campaign, Free Play and pass-and-play all run entirely on your device and send nothing anywhere.

Local multiplayer over Wi-Fi (iOS)

The iOS version can host a game between devices on the same local network. Your chosen player name and the state of the match are sent directly to the other devices playing, peer-to-peer. This traffic passes through no server, and none of it is recorded or retained outside the devices taking part. iOS asks your permission before HexFall can use the local network, and the feature does nothing until you allow it.

Playing online

Online multiplayer is optional, and none of this section applies if you do not use it. When you play an online match, HexFall uses Google Firebase (Realtime Database), and the following is transmitted:

  • Your anonymous identifier, described above, so that each player in the match can be told apart.
  • Your chosen player name, so that the other players in the match can see who they are playing against. You choose this name, and you are not required to use your real name.
  • Match state — the board, the moves and the results — exchanged between the players in that match while it is being played.
  • Your IP address, which is necessarily visible to Google's servers in order to deliver network traffic, as it is for any internet connection. It is not used to determine your location.

The Daily Challenge leaderboard

The Daily Challenge is a new game setup each day. To play it, the app downloads that day's challenge from Google Firebase; that download sends nothing about you except the anonymous identifier needed to read it.

Joining the leaderboard is optional. The first time you win a Daily Challenge you are asked for a name to appear under, and you can decline. If you do join, the following is stored with Google Firebase and linked to your anonymous identifier:

  • Your leaderboard name. It is also your profile name, and it must be unique. You choose it, and you are not required to use your real name.
  • Each Daily Challenge you win: your score, the number of turns you took, a breakdown of how the score was made up, and the time it was submitted.
  • The time you last used the leaderboard, so that a name nobody has used for 30 days can be released for someone else to claim.

Your leaderboard name, and the scores and turn counts you post, can be seen by everyone who plays HexFall, on iOS and Android. Top-three medals are worked out on your device from the public leaderboard, and nothing extra about them is stored online.

Names are checked against a list of words that are not allowed. The developer may remove a name, or a score, that breaks these rules or appears not to have been earned in play.

HexFall does not collect your email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files, or any payment details. There is no chat and no way to send messages to other players.

Purchases

HexFall offers a single in-app purchase that unlocks the full game. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple on iOS and by Google Play on Android. The developer never sees or receives your payment details or store account credentials — the app receives only confirmation that the purchase is valid.

Third parties

The only third-party service HexFall uses is Google Firebase, which acts as the developer's data processor for the anonymous identifier, match data, the daily challenge and the leaderboard. Google's handling of that data is covered by the Firebase Privacy and Security policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Independently of the app, Apple and Google may collect information about downloads, purchases and crashes. That collection is theirs, governed by their own privacy policies, and any reports the developer receives are aggregated and do not identify you.

How long information is kept

Match data exists for the duration of a match and is removed when the last player leaves. A match abandoned without a clean exit — for example if every device loses connection at once — may persist until it is removed; abandoned matches are cleared periodically, and you can request removal at any time using the contact address below.

Leaderboard entries and your leaderboard name are kept until you delete them. A leaderboard name that has not been used for 30 days may be claimed by another player.

The anonymous identifier stored on your device persists until you delete it, or until you uninstall the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time:

  • Delete Leaderboard Data, on the Profile screen once you have joined the leaderboard, removes your leaderboard name and every score you have posted, in one step. Your personal bests and streak stay on your device.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored information, including the anonymous identifier held on your device. It does not remove anything you have posted to the leaderboard — use Delete Leaderboard Data first, or email the address below.
  • To request deletion of any remaining online data associated with your anonymous account, email hexfall.game@gmail.com. Because the identifier is anonymous, please send your message from the device in question, or include the identifier, so that the correct record can be found. Requests are actioned within 30 days.

What HexFall does not do

  • No analytics, advertising or attribution software.
  • No tracking, and no selling of your data. Nothing is shared with anyone other than Google Firebase as described here — apart from what you choose to post to the public leaderboard.
  • No user accounts to create, and no sign-in — online play and the leaderboard use an anonymous identifier only.
  • No chat, and no user-generated content beyond the player name you choose.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

Children

HexFall is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Data protection law gives you rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. The information HexFall holds is limited to what is described above, and the Deleting your data section explains how to exercise those rights. Questions can be sent to the address below.

Changes to this policy

If HexFall gains a feature that changes any of the above, this policy will be updated and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about HexFall can be sent to hexfall.game@gmail.com.
