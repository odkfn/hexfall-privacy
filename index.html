HexFall — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

HexFall is a single-player and local multiplayer strategy game for iOS. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: HexFall does not collect any personal data. There is no account to create, no server behind the game, and no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Information stored on your device

HexFall saves your progress on your device only, using the operating system's standard app storage. That includes:

  • the player name you choose;
  • campaign progress, unlocked content and achievements;
  • game settings, such as sound and haptics preferences;
  • whether the full-game unlock has been purchased.

This information never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to the developer, and there is no way for the developer to see it. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Local multiplayer over Wi-Fi

HexFall can host a game between devices on the same local network. When you join or host such a game, your chosen player name and the state of the match are sent directly to the other devices playing, peer-to-peer over your local network.

This traffic does not pass through any server operated by the developer, and none of it is recorded or retained anywhere outside the devices taking part in that match. iOS will ask your permission before HexFall can use the local network, and the feature does nothing until you allow it.

Purchases

HexFall offers a single in-app purchase that unlocks the full game. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees or receives your payment details, card number or Apple Account credentials. The app only receives confirmation from Apple that the purchase is valid.

Apple's handling of purchase information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Information collected by Apple

Independently of this app, Apple may collect information about downloads, purchases and crashes, and — if you use TestFlight to test a pre-release version — additional diagnostic information. That collection is Apple's, governed by Apple's own privacy policy, and any crash or usage reports the developer receives from Apple are aggregated and do not identify you.

What HexFall does not do

  • No user accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or attribution software.
  • No third-party code libraries of any kind.
  • No tracking, and no sharing or selling of data — there is none to share or sell.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

Children

HexFall does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no information is collected at all, none is collected from children.

Your rights

Data protection law gives you rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. The developer holds no personal data about HexFall players, so there is nothing to access or erase on request. The information the game saves is under your own control on your device, and removing the app removes it.

Changes to this policy

If HexFall gains a feature that changes any of the above — a server, online leaderboards, or anything that sends information off your device to the developer — this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about HexFall can be sent to hexfall.game@gmail.com.
