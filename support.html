HexFall — Support

Turn-based strategy on a hex board.

Something broken, something confusing, or an idea for the game? Email hexfall.game@gmail.com and you'll get a reply from the person who made it.

If you're reporting a problem, it helps enormously to include your device (for example iPhone 16 or iPad 9th generation), your iOS version, and what you were doing when it went wrong.

I bought the full game, but it's locked again

Tap Multiplayer on the main menu, or Unlock Full Game on the campaign screen. Either opens the purchase screen, which has a Restore Purchases button at the bottom.

Your unlock is tied to the Apple Account that bought it, so make sure you're signed in with the same one. Restoring is free, and works on any device you sign into with that account.

I reinstalled and lost my campaign progress

Campaign progress, achievements and unlocked content are saved on your device, not in the cloud, so deleting the app deletes them. Your purchase is unaffected and can always be restored as above. Cloud saves aren't in the game yet.

Wi-Fi multiplayer can't find the other device

Things to check, in the order they usually go wrong:

  • Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network. Two devices on different networks, or one on mobile data, cannot see each other.
  • HexFall needs local network permission. iOS asks once, the first time you use multiplayer. If it was declined, switch it back on in Settings › HexFall › Local Network.
  • Both devices should be on the same version of HexFall.
  • Some public, guest and workplace networks block devices from talking to each other directly. A home network is the reliable case.

There is no server behind multiplayer — the devices connect to each other directly — so an internet connection isn't required, but a shared local network is.

How do I unlock more races, maps and boons?

By playing the campaign. Beating a level for the first time awards whatever that level is worth: a new board for Free Play, sometimes a new race, sometimes a new boon. Others are earned through achievements that track everything you've ever done across all your games.

What does the purchase include?

One purchase, made once, unlocks campaign levels 6 to 15 along with local pass-and-play and Wi-Fi multiplayer. It isn't a subscription, there's nothing else to buy, and it won't ask again.
